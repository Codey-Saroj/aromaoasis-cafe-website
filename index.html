    Our Story

    A journey of passion,flavour and community

    A Home Away From Home

    Founded in 2010, Aroma Oasis started as a small corner café with a big dream: to create a space where people could disconnect from the hustle of everyday life and reconnect with themselves and others over exceptional coffee.

    Our founders, Mr.Haze and Lara, traveled across the world to source the finest coffee beans, developing relationships with farmers who share our commitment to quality, sustainability, and ethical practices.

    Today, we're proud to be a beloved neighborhood institution, where regulars are greeted by name and newcomers quickly feel like they've been coming here for years. Every cup we serve carries our story—one of passion, perseverance, and the perfect brew.
